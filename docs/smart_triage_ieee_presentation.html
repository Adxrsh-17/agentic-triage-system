Clinical Motivation & Architectural Contributions
02 / 24
🚨 The Clinical Emergency Challenge
  • Emergency Department Overcrowding: Over 130 million annual ED visits in the US face severe intake bottlenecks, leading to delayed interventions.
  • Triage Cognitive Overload: High inter-rater variability and under-triage risks during emergency department patient surges.
  • Siloed Pre-Hospital Dispatch: Disconnect between patient intake and hospital capacity, delaying trauma bay preparation.
🏆 Key System Contributions
  • Deterministic Multi-Agent Pipeline: 6-node LangGraph StateGraph guaranteeing termination and execution safety.
  • Dual-Engine Clinical Consensus: Synergistic fusion of Infermedica v3 certified knowledge base with Groq LPU LLaMA-3-70B.
  • "Escalate, Never Downgrade": Strict mathematical consensus arbitration ensuring 100% emergency sensitivity.
  • Automated Resource Pre-Allocation: Real-time reservation of ER bays, care teams, and critical equipment prior to patient arrival.
